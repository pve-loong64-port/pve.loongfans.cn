Support

Proxmox VE Loong64 Port has been tested working on Loongson 2K3000, 3A6000, 3C6000/S, 3C6000/D, and 3C6000/Q.

Known Issues:

  • Running multiple virtual machines each with many cores (≥ 32 cores) under heavy loads may cause virtual machines to hang.
